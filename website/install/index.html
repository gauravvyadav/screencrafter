Thanks for installing Screencrafter!

You're all set to start capturing beautiful screenshots. Here's a quick guide to help you get the most out of the extension.

1

Pin the Extension

Click the puzzle icon in your Chrome toolbar and pin Screencrafter for quick access anytime.

2

Choose your Capture Mode

Click the Screencrafter icon to choose between Visible Area, Full Page, or Select Area capture.

3

Annotate & Export

Once captured, use the built-in editor to polish your screenshot and export as PNG or PDF.
